## 2026-10-01

**Jihyo** will be the main host of **D AWARDS 2027 in Paris** and will also give a solo performance. The awards take place on January 15–16, 2027 at **Plenitude Arena** (formerly Paris La Défense Arena), and will be the first K-content awards show held in the EU. Jihyo heads the first lineup, alongside DPR IAN, P1Harmony, KISS OF LIFE and AND2BLE.
